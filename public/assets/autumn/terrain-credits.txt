This artistic scene uses a cropped, rotated and rescaled elevation field from
the Swiss Alps (bounds 7.70863 to 7.84467 E, 46.43767 to 46.55094 N), with a blended boundary,
additional small rock relief, and fictional vegetation/snow/materials.
It is not a geographic reconstruction and is not suitable for navigation.
